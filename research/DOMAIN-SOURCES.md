## Interpretation boundaries

- The eight categories are verified. Their sidebar descriptions describe intended institutional use, not an invented rigid approval matrix. The guidelines’ broad eligibility definition does not establish an automatic institution-type-to-suffix mapping.
- Local eligibility only checks self-reported Indian location, recognized/affiliated/registered status, preparer authority and research evidence where applicable. Passing is explicitly preliminary.
- ASCII format checks enforce the published length/character rules. Reserved-name databases, institutional-name equivalence, misleading abbreviations and availability are not locally verified.
- IDNs receive a conservative Devanagari syntax/encoding precheck, explicitly not the registrar’s full IDN policy. No verified script tables or IDN API are available; official portal checks remain required.
- Local forms are a simplified preparation schema. Head/authorized contact name/designation/email/phone are supported by Annexure A; the four contact groups are supported by the guidelines. Individual admin/technical screens were not available publicly. Phone/email are draft checks, not assertions about exact server requirements. Institution website is optional context, never eligibility evidence. No Aadhaar, father’s name, age, signature image or payment credentials are collected.
- Government schools use the three-document exception. Other applicants receive standard documents plus applicable research, translation and GST evidence. This is a readiness checklist, not a claim of accepted documents.
- The guidelines reference Annexure I but no current separate public file/wording was verified. Custom declarations are labelled local preparation acknowledgements; the official declaration remains on the registry.
- No authorized official API, credentials, backend, database, availability feed, payment integration or tracking feed was supplied. Public destination URLs were verified; authenticated actions were not executed.
